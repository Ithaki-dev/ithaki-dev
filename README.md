## 👋 About me

Full Stack Developer from Costa Rica, focused on building solutions that connect **software development, infrastructure, and design**. I care about writing clean code, working with cloud-native tools, and delivering products that are reliable and pleasant to use.

- 🔭 Building full-stack applications with modern frameworks and strengthening my cloud/DevOps practice
- 🧠 Working with AI models and integrations (LLMs like Hermes) to build smarter, automated workflows
- ⚙️ Designing automation pipelines with **n8n** to connect apps, APIs, and internal tools
- 🖥️ Self-hosting and managing virtualized infrastructure with **Proxmox VE**
- 🗄️ Comfortable working across relational and non-relational databases
- 🛠️ Solid foundation in IT infrastructure, networking, scripting, and troubleshooting
- 🎨 Bringing a user-first, detail-oriented perspective to technical problems
